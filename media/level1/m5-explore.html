Noise on the Bell circuit

The Bell circuit (H, then CNOT) should give only 00 and 11. Add a gate error on the CNOT and readout errors on both qubits, run it, and compare the counts with the prediction and its 3σ shot-noise band. Then load the calibration data of a real IBM computer and compare with its published run.

Readout mitigation

If you know the readout matrix A, you can undo readout errors: solve A x = p for the measured probabilities p. Run one experiment to see the corrected numbers (they can be slightly negative), then repeat it 200 times to see what mitigation does to the average and to the spread.

The experiment

The true fraction of different results before readout is λ/2 (from the CNOT error). That is what mitigation tries to recover.
