From the order of a to the factors of N

Pick N and a number a with no factor in common with N. The boxes are ak mod N for k = 1, 2, 3, ...; the first 1 marks the order r. If r is even and h = ar/2 mod N is not N − 1, gcd(h − 1, N) and gcd(h + 1, N) are factors of N.

What order finding measures, and how to read it

The bars are the exact probabilities of each result m of the order-finding circuit with t counting qubits (started in |1⟩). Each result gives m/2ᵗ ≈ s/r for a random s. Press Take a shot: the table shows the continued-fraction estimate s/r and whether its denominator is the order.
