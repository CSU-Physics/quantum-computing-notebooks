Bias or shot noise: which method is best for your shots?

The mitigation capstone: ⟨X X ... X⟩ of a GHZ state, ideally 1, under the course noise model (P2 = 0.01, READOUT = 0.02). Each method has a bias (its exact value minus 1, from the density matrix) and a shot noise σ that falls as 1/√shots. ZNE splits the shots equally between scales 1, 3 and 5. The chart shows the root-mean-square error √(bias² + σ²) against the total number of shots.
