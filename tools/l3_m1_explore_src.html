Pure states on the sphere, mixtures inside

State A and state B are pure states in the x-z plane of the Bloch sphere (RY(θ)|0⟩). The qubit is A with probability w and B otherwise. Its density matrix is the same mixture of theirs, and its Bloch vector is the same mixture of their Bloch vectors.
