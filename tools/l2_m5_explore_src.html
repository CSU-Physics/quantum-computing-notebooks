Can you find the ground energy of H₂ by hand?

The ansatz of the lab: layer 1 is RY(θ₀) on qubit 0 and RY(θ₁) on qubit 1; layer 2 adds a CNOT, then RY(θ₂) and RY(θ₃). Move the angles to lower the energy ⟨ψ|H|ψ⟩. The exact lowest energy is −1.857275 hartree; chemical accuracy means within 1.6 millihartree.
