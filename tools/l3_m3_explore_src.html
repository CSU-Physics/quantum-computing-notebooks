What an echo can and cannot undo

A qubit starts in |+⟩, an arrow along X on the equator of the Bloch sphere, and waits 10 steps. Each arrow below is one shot. A detuning turns every arrow by the same angle per step; a spread gives each shot its own steady angle per step (it differs from shot to shot but not during the wait); fast noise kicks each arrow at random at every step. ⟨X⟩ is the average of cos φ over 400 shots. Switch the echo on: an X after 5 steps and another at the end.

Extrapolating to zero noise

Each CNOT multiplies ⟨XX⟩ by (1 − p). Folding runs the same circuit with 1, 3 or 5 CNOTs, so the measured value is E(λ) = E₀ (1 − p)^λ at noise scale λ. Fit the points and read the fit at λ = 0. The error bars show one standard deviation for the chosen number of shots per circuit.
